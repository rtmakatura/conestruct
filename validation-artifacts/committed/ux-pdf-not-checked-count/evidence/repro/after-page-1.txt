Taper Length
At 30 mph, below the 45 mph threshold, the taper length is the offset width times the speed squared, divided by 60.
L = 8 x 30^2 / 60 = 120 ft
L/3 = 120 / 3 = 40 ft
Field Value
Closure type shoulder
Offset (shoulder width) 8 ft (assumed)
Required taper (L/3 (shoulder taper)) 40 ft
Source: MUTCD 11th Ed. Sec 6B.08, Table 6B-4 (taper length L). Shoulder closures use L/3 per Sec 6B.08 (Table
6B-3).
CDOT reference: CDOT S-630-1 Case 11 (right-shoulder closure, applied to one-way street)
Buffer Space
MUTCD Table 6B-2 sets 200 ft; the CDOT supplement sets no value at this speed.
Source: MUTCD 11th Ed. Sec 6B.06, Table 6B-2 (stopping sight distance)
Channelizing Device Spacing
In the taper, devices are spaced 30 ft apart, the speed in feet at 30 mph (MUTCD Sec 6K.01).
Along the tangent, devices are spaced 60 ft apart, twice the speed in feet (MUTCD Sec 6K.01).
L/3 = 40 ft / 30 ft max spacing -> 4 drums at 13.3 ft intervals
300 ft / 60 ft max spacing -> 6 cones at 60.0 ft intervals
Buffer: 200 ft / 60 ft max spacing -> 3 cones at 50.0 ft intervals (CDOT S-630-1 Sheet 2 General Note 18b: tangents
along the buffer space)
Downstream taper: 50 ft at ~25 ft spacing -> 2 cones (MUTCD Sec 6B.08: 50-100 ft downstream taper); 6 tangent + 3
buffer + 2 downstream = 11 cones total
