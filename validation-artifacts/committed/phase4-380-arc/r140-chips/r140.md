# R140: the near-intersection chip rows at 44 px, every width

Light lane, one CSS rule. Before = production after `cc84890` (main); after = a local stack of
`r140-ni-chips`; both measured with `../tap-targets/tapsize.cjs`.

**The change:** `.workbench .band-stack .chip` leaves rule 15's list (32 px at the desk, 44 at the
phone) and takes R107's 44 px at every width. In production `.chip` renders only in the
near-intersection form's two rows; the two other forms that use it (lane closure, work beyond the
shoulder) are scenario kinds that aren't enabled.

| Control | 1440 before → after | 360 before → after |
|---|---|---|
| Cross-street directions: "One (T-intersection)", "Both" | 398 × 36 → **398 × 44** | ≥ 44 → unchanged |
| Cross-street lanes "1"–"4" | 199 × 36 → **199 × 44** | ≥ 44 → unchanged |

Every other control is the same size on both sides. The rows that appear on one side only in the
diff (a second lane row and the "Lane count is right" hold before; "Not set" speed after) are the
cross-street data the two runs read, not this change. Guard: `components/tap-targets.test.ts`
(R140 case).
