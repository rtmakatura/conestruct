# #382 R1 step 1: source search for a minimum work-area length

**Result: none exists.** Neither the MUTCD 11th Ed. Part 6 nor CDOT S-630-1 (July 2026) states a minimum work-area (work space) length or relates the work area's length to the taper's.

## What was searched
- **MUTCD 11th Ed. Part 6** (`validation-artifacts/ta10_flagger/mutcd_part6.pdf`, printed pages 765 onward). The full text was searched for "length of the work/activity area", "work area/space shall/should/may be", "minimum length", "minimum work area", "longer/shorter than the taper", and every "work area" or "work space" line that mentions length, feet, short, minimum or maximum. The only minimum lengths found are taper lengths (§6B.08 ¶12, ¶14) and barricade rail lengths (§6F).
- **CDOT S-630-1 (July 2026)** (`validation-artifacts/s630-1-2026.pdf`). Every one of the 48 "work area" lines was read. Its length statements cover emergency pull-offs ("For work areas greater than 1 mile in length, multiple emergency pull-off areas may...") and rolling-roadblock distances. None sets a minimum work-area length.

## The sections that would hold such a rule, and what they say
- **§6B.06 Activity Area, p. 773** (`sources/mutcd11-pdf009-printed773-6B06.txt`): defines the work space ("that portion of the highway closed to road users and set aside for workers, equipment, and material", ¶02). It also says "The work space may be stationary or may move as work progresses" (¶03). It gives no length. The only length guidance in the section is for the buffer: "the values shown in Table 6B-2 may be used to determine the length of the longitudinal buffer space" (¶11).
- **§6B.08 Tapers, pp. 775–776** (`sources/mutcd11-pdf011-012-printed775-776-6B08.txt`): Table 6B-3 gives the taper lengths. The one relation it states is between the taper and the transition: "A merging taper should be long enough to enable merging drivers to have adequate advance warning and sufficient length to adjust their speeds and merge into an adjacent lane before the downstream end of the transition" (¶06, p. 776). It says nothing about the work area.

So the limit stays unsourced, and under R1 it is dropped rather than replaced.
