## Setup Procedure

**Important: Set up from downstream to upstream (against traffic flow). This ensures you are always working behind protection.**

1. Position arrow board at the upstream start of the shoulder taper (station 1,250 ft). Set it to caution mode (flashing caution or alternating diamond). Shoulder work takes caution mode only, never an arrow (MUTCD 11th Ed. §6L.06 ¶18, p. 833; CDOT S-630-1 Sheet 2 General Note 26). Activate flashing.

2. Place the END ROAD WORK sign (G20-2) at the downstream termination (station -150 ft), right side of road 28 ft from centerline. Place matching sign on left side.

3. Place 2 traffic cones in the downstream taper, between station -50 ft (shoulder edge) and station 0 (lane edge) at 25 ft spacing. This taper returns traffic from the lane edge back to the shoulder edge past the work zone (MUTCD §6B.08 downstream taper). Then place 18 traffic cones along the right lane edge at 59 ft spacing, starting at station 0 (downstream end of work zone) and working upstream to station 1,000 ft. Offset: 24 ft from centerline. Then continue the line through the buffer space: 3 more traffic cones at 50 ft spacing, from station 1,000 ft up to the taper (station 1,200 ft), at the same offset. Keep the buffer itself clear of work and equipment (CDOT S-630-1 Sheet 2 General Note 18b: no more than twice the speed limit apart along the buffer space).

4. Place G20-5P WORK ZONE plaque(s) within the work zone at approximately 1,000 ft intervals. Place matching plaques on left side.

5. Place 3 channelizing drums in the shoulder taper, starting at station 1,200 ft and working upstream to station 1,250 ft. Space drums at 25 ft intervals. Drums transition from the lane edge (24 ft) to the shoulder edge (34 ft).

6. Place advance warning signs upstream of the taper:
   - W21-5aR at 100 ft upstream of taper start (station 1,350 ft), right side.
   - W20-2 at 200 ft upstream of taper start (station 1,450 ft), right side.
   - W20-1 at 300 ft upstream of taper start (station 1,550 ft), right side.
   Place matching signs on left side at each location.

## Takedown Procedure

**Important: Remove from upstream to downstream (with traffic flow). Remove advance warning signs first, work zone devices last.**

1. Remove advance warning signs: W20-1 first, then W20-2, then W21-5aR. Remove both sides at each station.
2. Remove taper drums from upstream to downstream.
3. Deactivate and remove arrow board.
4. Remove work zone cones from upstream to downstream.
5. Remove G20-5P plaques.
6. Remove G20-2 END ROAD WORK sign(s).

## Safety Notes

- All personnel in the work zone must wear ANSI Class 2 (daytime) or Class 3 (nighttime) high-visibility apparel.
- Divided highway: this plan posts signs on both sides of the roadway by choice. CDOT S-630-1 Sheet 2 General Note 8 doesn't require it when only one shoulder is closed (Case 11 on Sheet 7).
- Maintain 200 ft longitudinal buffer space between the taper and the work area at all times.
- Maximum speed reduction per sign installation: 15 mph (CDOT S-630-1 Sheet 2 General Note 3).
- This plan was generated by MHT Tool. A certified Traffic Control Supervisor (TCS) must review, approve, and sign before field implementation.
- Reference: CDOT S-630-1, MUTCD 11th Edition Part 6, Colorado Supplement (effective January 18, 2026).

## Pedestrian and Bicycle Accommodation

- A pedestrian facility is affected by this closure. 2 R9-9 SIDEWALK CLOSED signs are placed at the closure (MUTCD §6G.10: the plain R9-9 legend; no alternate-side assertion, which belongs to R9-10, and no detour-routing signs, which require sidewalk geometry this plan does not carry). Sidewalk closure points are barricaded (MUTCD §6C.02). Pedestrians must not be diverted into an open travel lane.
- **Denver TCP sheet requirements**: The submitted TCP must depict sidewalks, pedestrian accessible routes, and bike-lane assignments as existing conditions, plus the proposed pedestrian accessible route, bike facilities, and a sidewalk/bike closure detail. That existing-conditions inventory is site-survey content this generated draft doesn't carry. The TCS supplies it on the submitted TCP.
- The TCS verifies pedestrian and bicycle accommodation on site; where a facility is maintained, it must remain accessible and detectable per MUTCD standards.

## Emergency Access
