## Plan (written before the diff)

1. **Cause (investigation first).**
   - **Confirmed: the station is right and so is the map; the refusal reports it in a frame the operator can't see.** `work_start_cross_station` (`src/api/schemas.py:1471-1522`) measures in the layout frame, whose docstring says "0 = the work's downstream end, growing upstream" (`:1479-1480`). A work-start pin is the work zone's *upstream* edge: `_corridor_from_work_start` (`src/rules/corridor.py:1082-1084`) says "The pin is the work zone's upstream edge for the occupied lane's traffic, so in the corridor frame it sits at station ``downstream_taper_ft + work_zone_ft``", and the check subtracts `downstream_taper_ft` (`schemas.py:1510`), so the pin sits at station L. On Wadsworth northbound the work runs north (downstream) from the pin, which is what the picker draws. A cross street at the pin is therefore at station L, and one marked 13 ft north of it (13 ft into the work) is at L - 13, whatever L is. "Station 487 ft, work zone spans 0..500" is exactly "13 ft past where the work starts". The issue's first suspect (a reference-end mismatch between `along_station_ft` and the 0..L span) is ruled out: both use the same frame, and the predicate `0 <= station <= L` is right in it.
   - **Possible: where the 13 ft comes from.** The marked intersection is the raw click (`LocationPickerModal.tsx:787-789`, `placeCrossPin` stores `{ lat: qLat, lng: qLng }` with no snap), so 13 ft is most likely the click landing 13 ft north of the pin's place on the road. The live click coordinates weren't recorded, so this stays possible.
   - **Confirmed: no way forward.** None of the six cross-street-station refusals (schemas.py `:1512` inside, `:1518` too far, `:1068` the corridor_end variant; layout.py `:1758` near-side curb, `:1767` far-side curb, `:1776` the direct-caller fallback) names a move. Changing the length can't clear the Wadsworth case: the cross street stays 13 ft past the work start, so only a length under 13 ft would.

## Open edges (reviewer notes, not fixed)

- (a) "More than N ft" can fall short by under a foot: the station is rounded to the foot before the moves are computed, so N can be up to half a foot under the true overlap. Taking `math.ceil` of the unrounded value would close it.
- (b) The corridor_end 422's new sentence ("Move the work zone or change alongStationFt so the cross street falls outside 0..L") ignores the cross street's half-width, so a station just outside 0..L can still meet the generator's curb-line refusal. API-only (the frontend pre-validates corridor_end), low risk.
- (c) The curb-box messages describe the overlap with the same ceiling as the move: a 17.3 ft overlap reads "18 ft inside", not 17.
