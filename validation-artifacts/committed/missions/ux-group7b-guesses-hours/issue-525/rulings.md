## R145 self-ruled

- **R145-1 "The backend" is the road list's server producer.** The list is built only by `/api/road-bearing` (server side); group 8's R145-1 (`validation-artifacts/committed/missions/ux-group8-map-window/rulings.md`: "Grouping lives in the road list's server producer") already placed #507's grouping there (`lib/road-detection/group.ts`, called by the route). The same-street merge and the cross-street name go in the same place; the picker renders what it gets and groups nothing. No MUTCD value is computed (Rule 3 unaffected).
- **R145-2 Same street** is group 8's R145-2 test, which reads (`ux-group8-map-window/rulings.md:55`): "Two candidates in one lookup are one street when: same name (or same ref when unnamed), same OSM highway class, …". The merge takes its first two conditions: the same `name` (or the same `ref` when both are unnamed) **and the same `highway_class`**, as `dedup.ts` and `group.ts` both key. Exact strings, as those files compare them today; key normalisation is #501/#502, out of scope. *Corrected in gate round 2:* round 1 quoted this as name-only and the code dropped the class, so a same-name one-way `*_link` nearer the pin could lead and fold the mainline carriageway, leaving `groupCarriageways` unable to pair it with its opposite (reviewer finding 1; test in `same-street.test.ts`).
- **R145-3 Two opposite carriageways never merge.** Two one-way ways whose travel runs 150° or more apart (`OPPOSITE_MIN_DEG`, #308) are a divided road's two carriageways; group 8's grouping (`group.ts`) pairs them as `twin` after this step. The merge leaves them for it, so Colfax, Speer and Lincoln keep their one entry each exactly as today.
- **R145-4 Ways merge only when their travel reads the same** (Rule 10/12: an entry's travel words are its nearest way's, so they must hold for every way folded into it). Both two-way (oneway absent or `no`), or both one-way (`yes`/`-1`) with travel within `STITCH_HEADING_TOL_DEG` (60°). A street that turns one-way at the corner keeps two rows, each with its own true words. A `reversible`/`alternating` way never merges. CHOSEN (non-safety; it only decides which rows the list shows).
- **R145-5 "Connects"** = the two ways share an OSM node (the same vertex coordinates; Overpass sends a shared node's coordinates identically), counted through any chain of same-name ways the lookup found within the 30 m snap. Sourced from the data, no number.
- **R145-6 "Runs along the same line"** = axes within 25° (CHOSEN, reused: the picker's `PARALLEL_TOLERANCE_DEG` in `lib/road-detection/cross-street.ts` and the backend's `CROSS_STREET_PARALLEL_DEG`, the tool's existing "this way is the road itself" angle), and the other way's nearest point lies within 3 m of the lead way's line. 3 m is CHOSEN (non-safety): above the 1.4 m measured here between way 812978772's nearest point and the line of the lead way 1548629055's snap segment (the pieces' digitising wobble; trace.md), and under half the narrowest carriageway gap measured here (East Colfax's two carriageways, 6.6 m apart at this corner), so a divided road tagged two-way on both halves still lists two rows. The stitcher's 60 m / 60° / 150° don't fit this test: 60° and 60 m would fold a divided road's carriageways together.
- **R145-7 The distance is the nearest way's.** The list is sorted nearest first; a later way folds into the earlier, so the entry keeps the lead's way id, geometry, snap and distance. Nothing about the lead changes.
- **R145-8 "At an intersection with that street"** = another listed street (different name, not within 25° of this one's axis, so a same-direction fork or frontage road is never named) shares an OSM node with this street within 30 m of the pin. 30 m is reused: the route's snap limit and the backend's `CROSS_STREET_RADIUS_M`. The nearest such street is named. An unnamed one (no name or ref) is not named (Rule 10: "at Unnamed road" says nothing).
- **R145-9 Words.** The row's second line appends " · at <cross street>", e.g. "Traffic both ways · 1 m from your pin · at East Colfax Avenue". The cross street's name is OSM's `name` (or `ref`), as every other row names roads. The label only reads the list; the plan's cross street is still the picker's cross-street step and the backend's `cross_street_geometry`, unchanged.
- **R145-11 Implied one-way never folds as two-way (gate round 2, reviewer finding 5).** OSM implies one-way on `motorway` and `motorway_link`; the backend already refuses to call an untagged one two-way (`src/rules/corridor.py` `IMPLIED_ONE_WAY_CLASSES`, `travel_reading` → "unknown"). The merge mirrors that set (commented as a mirror; the backend stays authoritative for the words): an untagged way of those classes does not read as two-way, so it never folds a reversed piece. `oneway=no` on them still folds. #514 is the open issue for untagged motorways. Known limit, stated in the module comment: `junction=roundabout` is also implied one-way, but the route doesn't relay `junction`, so a roundabout isn't caught. Adding that tag to the wire is outside this item's churn.
- **R145-12 Cross streets run before the grouping, and that is right (reviewer finding 3).** `attachCrossStreets` runs in `buildResponse`, before `groupCarriageways`. The grouping keeps the lead as `{...lead, twin}`, so a grouped divided-road entry keeps its own `cross_street`. Both carriageways share one name key, so they read the same node set, and either would get the same answer. Seen in the replay: grouped East Colfax at Williams reads "at Williams Street"; grouped East Colfax at Pennsylvania reads "at Pennsylvania Street".
- **R145-10 Where the work's direction from the corner is decided (traced, not changed).** Under `pinModel="work_start"` the pin is the upstream end for the closed lane's traffic (ruling 9, `src/rules/corridor.py` `opposing_work_start`), and the side choice (`WorkPlacement.side` + `travel` with/against the relayed centerline, `src/api/schemas.py` `WorkPlacement`) sets which way that traffic runs, so the work runs from the pin in that direction for `workLen`. Near/far side of a marked intersection is computed by the backend from `meta.intersection` along the road (`schemas.py` `alongStationFt` comment, `NearIntersectionScenario._check_station_producer`; `render_api.py` `nearest_intersection` call). One list entry per street doesn't change any of this: the lead way's stitched centerline already ran through the corner. No new direction question is needed for this item.
