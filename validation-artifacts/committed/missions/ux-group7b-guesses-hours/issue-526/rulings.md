- **S27. How the backend scores hours after midnight (#532, the trace ruling 4 asks for).** `evaluate_hours_days` evaluates each day kind once (`src/rules/jurisdiction.py:601`, `kinds = {k: _day_kind_eval(windows, ctx, k) for k in _DAY_KINDS}`). Inside `_day_kind_eval`, the shift splits at midnight into two pieces (`:537`, `_schedule_segments`; 8 pm to 5 am is 20-24 and 0-5), and **both pieces are scored against that same day kind's windows** (`:538-543`). The day a shift is counted under is its start day (`job.kind_dates`, `:618`, `_day_kind_of(d)` on each work date). So a Fri 8 pm to Sat 5 am shift is checked against weekday windows only, Sat's 0-5 included; a Sun 8 pm to Mon 5 am shift against weekend windows only. This is the same convention `evaluate_hours` (`hours_eval`) has used since #188 (`:320-324`: "Both segments evaluate against the work date's OWN day-kind — chosen (rule 12) to match the data convention, where a midnight-crossing window's continuation carries the same `days` tag as its opening segment (Denver's 0–5 continuation is tagged "weekday")"). **Is it right?** For the night windows on record, yes. Every record window touching midnight (read from `data/jurisdictions/*.json`): Denver 20-24 + 0-5 (weekday; the 0-5 piece's note is "Night-window continuation."), Centennial 19-24 + 0-5 (weekday; "Night-window continuation."), and Castle Rock's noise-ordinance bans 19-24 + 0-7 (weekday) and 18-24 + 0-8 (weekend). Denver's and Centennial's after-midnight pieces are tagged as continuations of the opening night, so scoring a shift's after-midnight hours under its start day reads them as written. **Castle Rock is the exception.** Its 0-7 and 0-8 pieces read as the calendar morning's ban, not as a continuation. So a shift running past 7 am on a weekend morning that started the evening before on a weekday is scored against the weekday ban. Example: Fri 8 pm to Sat 7:30 am. Sat 7-7:30 is then scored as allowed, though Saturday's ban runs to 8 am. The same applies to `hours_eval`. This is a scoring change, and `hours_eval` feeds the PDF, the tier ledger and the exposure figure (S1: park list). So it is not changed here.
