## The plan

1. **Problem.**
   - `conestruct/site/lib/search-coords.ts:39-43`: one half of a pair is a number with an optional degree sign and an optional letter, and the two halves may be separated by a space alone. So `39° 44.4` reads as the half `39°`, a space, and the half `44.4`: `{ kind: "point", lat: 39, lng: 44.4 }` (`:93`). `:63-64` drops one pair of brackets first (#544), so `(39° 44.4)` and `[39° 44.4]` read the same.
   - The same reading takes `39 44.4 N` and `N 39 44.4` (one letter, a space between) as a pair.
   - `:69`: text that is not a pair is refused only when it has a degree sign. `39.7401 N` or `44.4' N` (a hemisphere letter or a minutes mark, no degree sign) goes to the geocoder.
   - Both senders (`components/LocationPickerModal.tsx:1173` and `:1314`) place the pin from a `point` without asking.
2. **Rulings.** Ruling 1 and ruling 3 of `docs/prompts/cc-prompt-coords-wrong-pin.md` (Ryan, 2026-11-23), quoted in `rulings.md`.
3. **Change.**
   - Parser: with only a space between the two halves, both halves must carry the same marks (a degree sign on both or on neither, a letter on both or on neither). Otherwise the text is refused (`unreadable`).
   - Parser: a half may carry minutes and seconds (`39° 44.406' N`, `39°44'24"N`). A pair of such halves is read when each has its degree sign, its marks and its hemisphere letter, the degrees are whole, minutes and seconds are under 60. Anything short of that is refused.
   - Parser: text that is not a pair, has no other words, and carries a minutes or seconds mark after a number, or a hemisphere letter with a decimal point, a comma or a sign, is refused. Before, it was geocoded.
   - Window: the one line for refused coordinates is reworded (`COORDS_UNREADABLE`). No second line, no new element, no CSS.
