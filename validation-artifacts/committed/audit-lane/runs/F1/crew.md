## Setup Procedure

**Important: Set up from downstream to upstream (against traffic flow). This ensures you are always working behind protection.**

1. Identify both flagger station positions (do not staff yet; stations are staffed after all devices are in place, step 7): Station #1 at station 555 ft (approach side, 100 ft upstream of the taper start, closed-lane side); Station #2 at station -300 ft (opposing approach, 300 ft past the work-area end per CDOT S-630-1 Case 17).

2. Place the END ROAD WORK sign (G20-2) at the downstream termination (station -150 ft), right side of road 16 ft from centerline. Place the opposing-direction G20-2 past the upstream end of the work zone (left side).

3. Place 4 traffic cones in the downstream taper, between station -50 ft (right lane edge) and station 0 (centerline) at 12 ft spacing. This taper returns right-direction traffic from the centerline back to the right lane edge past the work area (MUTCD §6B.08 ¶12; S-630-1 Case 17 annotates the run 100' MAX.). Then place 5 traffic cones along the centerline boundary between the open opposing lane and the closed work lane at 50 ft spacing, starting at station 0 (downstream end of work zone) and working upstream to station 200 ft. Then continue the line through the buffer space: 3 more traffic cones at 39 ft spacing, from station 200 ft up to the taper (station 355 ft), at the same offset. Keep the buffer itself clear of work and equipment (CDOT S-630-1 Sheet 2 General Note 18b: no more than twice the speed limit apart along the buffer space).

4. Place G20-5P WORK ZONE plaque(s) within the work zone at approximately 200 ft intervals.

5. Place 6 channelizing drums in the one-lane two-way taper, starting at station 355 ft and working upstream to station 455 ft. Space drums at 20 ft intervals (approximately 20 ft per MUTCD §6B.08. This taper stages stopped traffic behind the flagger; it must be short enough to not be mistaken for a transition). Drums transition from the right lane edge (12 ft) to the centerline (0 ft).

6. Place advance warning signs upstream of the taper:
   - W20-7 at 200 ft upstream of taper start (station 655 ft), right side.
   - W3-4 at 300 ft upstream of taper start (station 755 ft), right side.
   - W20-4 at 400 ft upstream of taper start (station 855 ft), right side.
   - W20-1 at 500 ft upstream of taper start (station 955 ft), right side.
   Repeat the series for the opposing approach (left side, negative stations; S-630-1 Case 17: "SIGN SEQUENCE IS THE SAME FOR OPPOSITE DIRECTION").

7. Staff both flagger stations (STOP/SLOW paddles, high-visibility apparel, radio or agreed hand-signal communication between stations). Verify each station is visible to approaching traffic from the full advance-warning distance and has an identified escape path. Begin the alternating one-way operation.

## Takedown Procedure

**Important: The one-lane operation ends before its warning signs come down. Flaggers control traffic until the roadway is fully restored to two-way operation.**

1. Complete work and clear all personnel and equipment from the work area.
2. Under continued flagger control, remove the work-zone cones and the taper drums (restoring the closed lane).
3. Cease the alternating one-way operation and release both flagger stations.
4. Remove advance warning signs on both approaches: W20-1 first, then W20-4, then W3-4, then W20-7.
5. Remove G20-5P plaques and the G20-1/G20-2 BEGIN/END ROAD WORK signs.

## Safety Notes

- All personnel in the work zone must wear ANSI Class 2 (daytime) or Class 3 (nighttime) high-visibility apparel.
- Maintain 155 ft longitudinal buffer space between the taper and the work area at all times.
- Maximum speed reduction per sign installation: 15 mph (CDOT S-630-1 Sheet 2 General Note 3).

## Pedestrian and Bicycle Accommodation

- A pedestrian facility is affected by this closure. 2 R9-9 SIDEWALK CLOSED signs are placed at the closure (MUTCD §6G.10: the plain R9-9 legend; no alternate-side assertion, which belongs to R9-10, and no detour-routing signs, which require sidewalk geometry this plan does not carry). Sidewalk closure points are barricaded (MUTCD §6C.02). Pedestrians must not be diverted into an open travel lane.
- **Denver TCP sheet requirements**: The submitted TCP must depict sidewalks, pedestrian accessible routes, and bike-lane assignments as existing conditions, plus the proposed pedestrian accessible route, bike facilities, and a sidewalk/bike closure detail. That existing-conditions inventory is site-survey content this generated draft doesn't carry. The TCS supplies it on the submitted TCP.
- The TCS verifies pedestrian and bicycle accommodation on site; where a facility is maintained, it must remain accessible and detectable per MUTCD standards.
