## Setup Procedure

**Important: Set up from downstream to upstream (against traffic flow). This ensures you are always working behind protection.**

1. Position arrow board at the upstream start of the merging taper (station 865 ft), centered in the closed left lane. Set to RIGHT ARROW mode (traffic merges right, out of the closed lane). Activate flashing.

2. Place the END ROAD WORK sign (G20-2) at the downstream termination (station -312 ft), left side of road 48 ft from the right edge of the roadway. Place matching sign on the right curb, 4 ft right of the right edge of the roadway.

3. Run the closed-lane cone line from the upstream curb line of the cross street (station -88 ft, past the downstream end of the work zone) up to station 0: 2 traffic cones at approximately 44 ft spacing. The closure extends to the corner and the lane reopens beyond the intersection; place no devices inside the intersection itself, curb line to curb line (MUTCD Fig. 6P-21 / S-630-1 Case 18). Then place 10 traffic cones along the closed-lane line through the work zone at 56 ft spacing, starting at station 0 (downstream end of work zone) and working upstream to station 500 ft. Offset: 33 ft from centerline. Then continue the line through the buffer space: 3 more traffic cones at 50 ft spacing, from station 500 ft up to the taper (station 700 ft), at the same offset. Keep the buffer itself clear of work and equipment (CDOT S-630-1 Sheet 2 General Note 18b: no more than twice the speed limit apart along the buffer space).

4. Place G20-5P WORK ZONE plaque(s) within the work zone at approximately 500 ft intervals.

5. Place 7 channelizing drums in the merging taper, starting at station 700 ft and working upstream to station 865 ft. Space drums at 28 ft intervals. Drums transition from the lane edge (44 ft) to the lane line (33 ft), closing the left lane.

6. Place advance warning signs upstream of the taper:
   - W4-2L at 100 ft upstream of taper start (station 965 ft), left side.
   - W20-5L at 200 ft upstream of taper start (station 1,065 ft), left side.
   - W20-1 at 300 ft upstream of taper start (station 1,165 ft), left side.
   Place matching signs on the right curb at each location, 4 ft right of the right edge of the roadway (CDOT S-630-1 Sheet 2 General Note 8: both sides on a one-way street).

7. Place the mainline regulatory and bookend signs, on both sides: the left side 48 ft from the right edge of the roadway, the right curb 4 ft right of it: R2-10 BEGIN DOUBLE FINES ZONE at station 1,115 ft (half the C spacing inside the outermost W20-1, per S-630-1 Case 18); G20-1 at station 600 ft; R2-11 END DOUBLE FINES ZONE at station -212 ft (downstream, past the lane reopening).

8. Place the cross-street advance sets, one set per approach leg. Stations for each set are measured along that leg FROM the intersection curb line, increasing away from the intersection:
   - **cross_a approach** (30 mph, Urban (low-speed)): W20-1 ROAD WORK AHEAD at 200 ft and R2-10 BEGIN DOUBLE FINES ZONE at 100 ft, both on the approach side 16 ft from that road's centerline. The W20-1 sits at twice the key distance A, the R2-10 at A, where the S-630-1 Sheet 10 advance-signing key gives A = 100 ft for this leg's own speed and road type. R2-11 END DOUBLE FINES ZONE at 100 ft on the departure side (facing traffic leaving the intersection). This approach is signalized: signal operation must be reviewed before work begins (MUTCD §6N.12; Part 4). See the audit trail.
   - **cross_b approach** (30 mph, Urban (low-speed)): W20-1 ROAD WORK AHEAD at 200 ft and R2-10 BEGIN DOUBLE FINES ZONE at 100 ft, both on the approach side 16 ft from that road's centerline. The W20-1 sits at twice the key distance A, the R2-10 at A, where the S-630-1 Sheet 10 advance-signing key gives A = 100 ft for this leg's own speed and road type. R2-11 END DOUBLE FINES ZONE at 100 ft on the departure side (facing traffic leaving the intersection). This approach is signalized: signal operation must be reviewed before work begins (MUTCD §6N.12; Part 4). See the audit trail.

**Sequencing note:** The MUTCD does not prescribe the order of mainline versus cross-street setup. Whether the cross-street sets go up before, after, or interleaved with the mainline sequence is the Traffic Control Supervisor's determination for this site.

## Takedown Procedure

**Important: Remove from upstream to downstream (with traffic flow). Remove advance warning signs first, work zone devices last.**

1. Remove advance warning signs: W20-1 first, then W20-5L, then W4-2L.
2. Remove taper drums from upstream to downstream.
3. Deactivate and remove arrow board.
4. Remove work zone cones from upstream to downstream.
5. Remove G20-5P plaques.
6. Remove G20-2 END ROAD WORK sign(s).
7. Remove the mainline R2-10 / R2-11 DOUBLE FINES ZONE pair and the G20-1 sign.
8. Remove each cross-street advance set (W20-1, R2-10, R2-11 per approach). The order of mainline versus cross-street takedown is the Traffic Control Supervisor's determination.

## Safety Notes

- All personnel in the work zone must wear ANSI Class 2 (daytime) or Class 3 (nighttime) high-visibility apparel.
- One-way street: all warning and regulatory signs are posted on BOTH sides of the roadway per CDOT S-630-1 Sheet 2 General Note 8. Offsets are measured from the right edge of the roadway.
- Closed lane: this plan draws the closure on the LEFTMOST lane. That is a modeling assumption, not an MUTCD rule; the work location dictates the lane. If the work occupies a different lane, this plan does not apply as drawn.
- Maintain 200 ft longitudinal buffer space between the taper and the work area at all times.
- Maximum speed reduction per sign installation: 15 mph (CDOT S-630-1 Sheet 2 General Note 3).
- This plan was generated by MHT Tool. A certified Traffic Control Supervisor (TCS) must review, approve, and sign before field implementation.
- Reference: CDOT S-630-1, MUTCD 11th Edition Part 6, Colorado Supplement (effective January 18, 2026).
