## The options

| option | what changes | advance signs shown | device types shown |
|---|---|---|---|
| **today** | the shipped layout | 2 of 5 (+3 MORE) | 17 of 19 (+2 MORE) |
| **A: one type ramp** | Same widths. Box titles are all 9 pt bold on one baseline with a hairline under each. The device summary's title moves up to that baseline. A hairline and 3 pt of air set the Reference/disclosure stack apart from the tables. | 2 of 5 | 19 of 19 |
| **B: A + legal trailer in the title block** | The Reference line, the GENERATED BY CONESTRUCT DRAFT line and "Verify all dimensions before use." move to the title block's unused foot, under STATUS. NOTES keeps the plan-specific disclosures. | **5 of 5** | 19 of 19 |
| **C: A + rebalanced widths** | Column weights change from 0.85/1.30/0.95/0.90 to 0.70/1.45/0.95/0.90. The legend's footnote wraps one word sooner. | 2 of 5 | 19 of 19 |

What each option shows:
- **A** tidies the hierarchy, but the stack under Reference stays packed against the NOTES floor.
- **B** is the only option that frees the NOTES box. The full advance series fits, and the legal trailer reads as
  part of the sheet's identity block, next to "DRAFT: Not for stamping".
- **C** shows that width is not the lever: NOTES is short of height, not width.

Two costs of B:
- The Reference line wraps to two lines inside the narrower title block.
- The DRAFT line also wraps there ("…NOT A SEALED / PLAN.").

Neither line's text changes. The crew PDF keeps its own reference line. B's moves can combine with C's widths.

In A, B and C the device summary gains 4 pt and shows all 19 types, so its bid line sits closer to the box floor.
The harness still measures it inside the box.
