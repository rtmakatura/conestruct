## Setup Procedure

**Important: Set up from downstream to upstream (against traffic flow). This ensures you are always working behind protection.**

1. Position arrow board at the upstream start of the shoulder taper (station 1,394 ft). Set to RIGHT ARROW mode (for right shoulder closure). Activate flashing.

2. Place the END ROAD WORK sign (G20-2) at the downstream termination (station -150 ft), right side of road 46 ft from centerline. Place matching sign on left side.

3. Place 2 traffic cones in the downstream taper, between station -50 ft (shoulder edge) and station 0 (lane edge) at 25 ft spacing — this taper returns traffic from the lane edge back to the shoulder edge past the work zone (MUTCD §6B.08 downstream taper). Then place 14 traffic cones along the right lane edge at 77 ft spacing, starting at station 0 (downstream end of work zone) and working upstream to station 1,000 ft. Offset: 42 ft from centerline.

4. Place G20-5P WORK ZONE plaque(s) within the work zone at approximately 1,000 ft intervals. Place matching plaques on left side.

5. Place 4 channelizing drums in the shoulder taper, starting at station 1,305 ft and working upstream to station 1,394 ft. Space drums at 30 ft intervals. Drums transition from the lane edge (42 ft) to the shoulder edge (52 ft).

6. Place advance warning signs upstream of the taper:
   - W21-5aR at 100 ft upstream of taper start (station 1,494 ft), right side.
   - W20-2 at 200 ft upstream of taper start (station 1,594 ft), right side.
   - W20-1 at 300 ft upstream of taper start (station 1,694 ft), right side.
   Place matching signs on left side at each location.

## Takedown Procedure

**Important: Remove from upstream to downstream (with traffic flow). Remove advance warning signs first, work zone devices last.**

1. Remove advance warning signs: W20-1 first, then W20-2, then W21-5aR. Remove both sides at each station.
2. Remove taper drums from upstream to downstream.
3. Deactivate and remove arrow board.
4. Remove work zone cones from upstream to downstream.
5. Remove G20-5P plaques.
6. Remove G20-2 END ROAD WORK sign(s).

## Safety Notes

- All personnel in the work zone must wear ANSI Class 2 (daytime) or Class 3 (nighttime) high-visibility apparel.
- Divided highway: All warning and regulatory signs must be posted on BOTH sides of the roadway per CDOT S-630-1 Sheet 2 General Note 8.
- Maintain 305 ft longitudinal buffer space between the taper and the work area at all times.
- Maximum speed reduction per sign installation: 15 mph (CDOT S-630-1 Sheet 2 General Note 3).
- This plan was generated by MHT Tool. A certified Traffic Control Supervisor (TCS) must review, approve, and sign before field implementation.
- Reference: CDOT S-630-1, MUTCD 11th Edition Part 6, Colorado Supplement (effective January 18, 2026).

## Pedestrian and Bicycle Accommodation

- No pedestrian or bicycle facility is flagged as affected by this closure. If a sidewalk, crosswalk, shared-use path, or bike facility exists within the work zone in the field, stop and have the TCS update the plan before setup.
- The TCS verifies pedestrian and bicycle accommodation on site; where a facility is maintained, it must remain accessible and detectable per MUTCD standards.

## Emergency Access

- All travel lanes remain open — the closure occupies the shoulder only. Emergency vehicles pass through the work zone in the open lanes.
- The Traffic Control Supervisor completes and confirms the emergency access plan for this site and coordinates notification of police, fire, and emergency medical responder agencies prior to work (TCS duties per CDOT Standard Specifications 630.11).
- An emergency access plan is a required minimum element of the approved Method of Handling Traffic (CDOT 630.10(a)); this section is draft input for it, not the approved plan.

## Fines Double Operational Rules

CDOT S-630-1 (July 2026) Sheet 12, Fines Double Signing Notes. The Fines Double envelope
(W3-5 advisory-speed sign(s) upstream of R2-10, entrance R2-1
posting the reduced limit, R2-10, R2-11, G20-5P/R2-6P assemblies,
and the downstream R2-1 posted-speed restoration) ships with this
plan because the work-zone posted speed is reduced below the
nominal posted speed. Field crew follows the rules below; engineer
may adjust placement spacing per Sheet 2 General Note 4.

- **S-630-1 Sheet 12, Note 1** — Install Fines Double signs no more than 4 hours before the start of the work day. Do not leave signs up overnight when work is not active.
- **S-630-1 Sheet 12, Note 2** — Remove or cover Fines Double signs when work concludes; doubled fines apply only when workers or work activity are present in the zone.
- **S-630-1 Sheet 12, Note 3** — Relocate the R2-10/R2-11 envelope to follow the actual work area as the project progresses; do not leave Fines Double signs in place beyond the active work zone.
- **S-630-1 Sheet 12, Note 4** — Maintain a 250 ft minimum spacing between Fines Double signs (R2-10, R2-11, G20-5P/R2-6P assemblies) and other warning or regulatory signs. Engineer may adjust placement to satisfy this minimum.
