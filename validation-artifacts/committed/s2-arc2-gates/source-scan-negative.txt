FINDINGS (verified by subject, not by number):
1. MUTCD Part 6 assigns NO distance to "near an intersection" for any
   traffic-control purpose.  The only numeric signal distances found are
   different subjects: temporary-signal interconnection within 1/4 mile of
   an adjacent signal, and the 200-ft grade-crossing preemption rule (both
   PDF p61).  Section 6N.12 ("Work within the Traveled Way at an
   Intersection", PDF p84-85 / printed 848-849) governs work AT an
   intersection and defines no proximity band.
2. S-630-1 assigns no distance either.  Sheet 2 Note 21 (PDF p150) is
   qualitative authority on the flagger half of the subject -- quoted:
   "Flagging operations shall not override visible regulatory signing or
   traffic signals, even when traffic signals have been disabled or set to
   flash mode. Only a uniformed police officer may direct traffic in a
   signalized intersection."  It is cited as qualitative basis ONLY,
   expressly not as a source for any number.

support point: 26.84 m at Colfax/Williams (live-signal-capture.txt).
