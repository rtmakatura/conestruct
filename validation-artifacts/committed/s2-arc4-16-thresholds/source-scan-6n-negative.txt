FINDINGS (all negative for the claim; positives beside each negative):

1. Section 6N.12 exists and is titled "Work within the Traveled Way at
   an Intersection" (printed p. 848, PDF 0-based p. 83; body runs
   printed 848-850).  POSITIVE: the section governs work at/adjacent to
   intersections — the flag's subject holds.  NEGATIVE: the section
   assigns NO proximity distance of any kind; the strings "250", "500",
   and "A-spacing" do not occur in its text (distance scan over PDF

   /\b500\b/).  This re-confirms s2-arc2's independent negative record
   (s2-arc2-gates/source-scan-negative.txt, finding 1: "Section 6N.12
   ... defines no proximity band").

2. Section 6N.16 exists and is titled "Interchanges" (printed p. 851,
   PDF 0-based p. 86).  POSITIVE: its ¶01 is ramp access + early
   coordination — the audit label's "ramp-access and coordination

   section contains NO distance anywhere (its three paragraphs cite
   Figures 6P-42/43/44 only).  The lone "500" hit in the scanned range
   sits in §6N.19 Late Merge signing — a different subject.

3. "Urban A-spacing 250 ft" does not exist.  Table 6B-1 (Recommended
   Advance Warning Sign Minimum Spacing, printed p. 773, PDF 0-based
   p. 8) rows: Urban (low speed) A=100 ft; Urban (high speed) A=350 ft;
   Rural A=500 ft; Expressway/Freeway A=1,000 ft.  No 250 ft row.  The
   500 ft interchange tolerance coincides with the RURAL A value — a
   coincidence, not a citation.

   warning signs generally, not an interchange-signing chapter.  The
   rendered "+ Ch. 6H — work zone signing for interchanges and ramp
   areas" descriptor misdescribed it.
