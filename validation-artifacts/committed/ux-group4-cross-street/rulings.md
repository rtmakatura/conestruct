## R145 self-ruled calls (CC, 2026-11-04)

- **X1, ruling 1's second sentence holds; kept verbatim.** `generate_near_intersection` lays "a per-approach advance warning set on every cross-street leg (§6N.12.06)" (`src/generation/layout.py:1953-1954`), and `scenario_to_call` passes every `scenario.approaches` leg to it (`src/api/schemas.py:1833-1847`). The legs come from the crossing's lookup (`approachesFromCrossStreet`, `conestruct/site/lib/road-detection/cross-street.ts:197-230`).
- **X2, ruling 2's trace and the order: side → kind → cross street.** The side options do not depend on the crossing. `_side_options` (`src/api/render_api.py:1594-1657`) reads the confirmed centerline, the pin, `meta.roadDirection` and `left_side_built(scenario)` (the kind and the road), never `meta.intersection`. The geometry read computes them before its not-marked check (`render_api.py:1738` then `1740-1743`), and its docstring says so: "the side options are listed (they are true) but nothing is laid out" (`render_api.py:1697-1701`). Today's "Mark the cross street…" in the side question is the frontend hiding true options (`components/bands/WhereQuestions.tsx:117-135, 157-158`). The dependency runs the other way: the crossing's station needs the side (`work_start_cross_station`, `src/api/schemas.py:1477-1500`). So the cross-street question comes after the kind, as the third question, and the side question shows its choices while the crossing is unmarked.
- **X3, the tap becomes a coordinate on the backend.** The aerial is a backend-drawn Static Images picture (`src/rendering/static_aerial.py:211-248`), not a map. The only frame whose centre and zoom the backend fixes is the `pin` stage (centred on the work pin at `PIN_ZOOM` + the zoom step, `static_aerial.py:238-239`); every other zoom-0 frame is Mapbox's `auto` fit. So, while the cross-street question is the card's current question, the aerial asks for the `pin` stage, and the tap's pixel goes to a new backend read, `POST /render/aerial-point` (pin, frame size, zoom step, tap x/y → lat/lng), computed by one function beside `PIN_ZOOM` in `static_aerial.py`, behind a thin Next proxy `/api/aerial-point`. No coordinate is computed on the frontend (Rule 3; #301 ruling 1, "drawn on the backend").
- **X4, one producer.** The picker's click handler (`LocationPickerModal.tsx` `placeCrossPin`: `/api/road-bearing` → the `scan_status` guard → `deriveCrossStreet`) moves into one function both callers use. The picker Save's cross-street apply (`GeneratorSidebar.tsx` `onPickerSave`: `meta.intersection`, `approachesFromCrossStreet`, `withoutOperatorSet(approachSpeed)`, `detectionOverrides: undefined`, the lane-count hold, the fresh-content guard) moves into one function both the Save and the band tap call. The wire fields are the ones the picker writes today; nothing new rides the scenario.

"Two-way street" / "One-way street": `scenario.approaches.length` (2 or 1, `approachesFromCrossStreet`'s `legCount` rule).
