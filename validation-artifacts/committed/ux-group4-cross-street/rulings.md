## R145 self-ruled calls on the revised ruling 2 (CC, 2026-11-04, session 2)

- **X20, the order and which kinds change the side options.** The card asks kind → cross street (near-intersection only) → side. The side options read the kind only through `left_side_built(scenario)` (`src/api/schemas.py:1316-1350`, called at `src/api/render_api.py:1621`). It is true only for shoulder work and near-intersection on a confirmed one-way STREET tagged one-way. So: on a confirmed one-way street, switching between flaggers and shoulder or near-intersection changes the options (flaggers drops the left edge). Shoulder ↔ near-intersection leaves them as they are. On a two-way road, a divided carriageway, or with no confirmed road (the four headings), no kind change alters them. The frontend computes none of this (Rule 3). It compares the backend's built `side_options` labels from before and after the kind change, and only once the geometry answer for the new kind has arrived (a new geometry object, so the stale one in the first render after the change never counts).
- **X21, the cleared side's line.** When X20 clears the side, the side question shows Ruling 2's line, "The sides changed with the new kind of work.", as a plain note (no ⚠; nothing is wrong, it says why the question is back). The line goes when the side is answered again. Clearing drops `meta.work` the same way the side's Undo does (#439 W6), so no side is written in its place. Supersedes #300/#315's "nothing is reset" for this one case: a kind change made in this card. A stored left side the backend refuses for any other reason still shows the backend's line and nothing is reset.
