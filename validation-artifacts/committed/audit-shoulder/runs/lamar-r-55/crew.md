## Setup Procedure

**Important: Set up from downstream to upstream (against traffic flow). This ensures you are always working behind protection.**

1. Position arrow board at the upstream start of the shoulder taper (station 878 ft). Set it to caution mode (flashing caution or alternating diamond). Shoulder work takes caution mode only, never an arrow (MUTCD 11th Ed. §6L.06 ¶18, p. 833; CDOT S-630-1 Sheet 2 General Note 26). Activate flashing.

2. Place the END ROAD WORK sign (G20-2) at the downstream termination (station -150 ft), right side of road 28 ft from centerline. Place matching sign on left side.

3. Place 2 traffic cones in the downstream taper, between station -50 ft (shoulder edge) and station 0 (lane edge) at 25 ft spacing. This taper returns traffic from the lane edge back to the shoulder edge past the work zone (MUTCD §6B.08 downstream taper). Then place 3 traffic cones along the right lane edge at 100 ft spacing, starting at station 0 (downstream end of work zone) and working upstream to station 200 ft. Offset: 24 ft from centerline.

4. Place G20-5P WORK ZONE plaque(s) within the work zone at approximately 200 ft intervals. Place matching plaques on left side.

5. Place 5 channelizing drums in the shoulder taper, starting at station 695 ft and working upstream to station 878 ft. Space drums at 46 ft intervals. Drums transition from the lane edge (24 ft) to the shoulder edge (34 ft).

6. Place advance warning signs upstream of the taper:
   - W21-5aR at 350 ft upstream of taper start (station 1,228 ft), right side.
   - W20-2 at 700 ft upstream of taper start (station 1,578 ft), right side.
   - W20-1 at 1,050 ft upstream of taper start (station 1,928 ft), right side.
   Place matching signs on left side at each location.

## Takedown Procedure

**Important: Remove from upstream to downstream (with traffic flow). Remove advance warning signs first, work zone devices last.**

1. Remove advance warning signs: W20-1 first, then W20-2, then W21-5aR. Remove both sides at each station.
2. Remove taper drums from upstream to downstream.
3. Deactivate and remove arrow board.
4. Remove work zone cones from upstream to downstream.
5. Remove G20-5P plaques.
6. Remove G20-2 END ROAD WORK sign(s).

## Safety Notes

- All personnel in the work zone must wear ANSI Class 2 (daytime) or Class 3 (nighttime) high-visibility apparel.
- Divided highway: All warning and regulatory signs must be posted on BOTH sides of the roadway per CDOT S-630-1 Sheet 2 General Note 8.
- Maintain 495 ft longitudinal buffer space between the taper and the work area at all times.
- Maximum speed reduction per sign installation: 15 mph (CDOT S-630-1 Sheet 2 General Note 3).
- This plan was generated by MHT Tool. A certified Traffic Control Supervisor (TCS) must review, approve, and sign before field implementation.
- Reference: CDOT S-630-1, MUTCD 11th Edition Part 6, Colorado Supplement (effective January 18, 2026).
