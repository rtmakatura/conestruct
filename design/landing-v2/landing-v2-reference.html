05EVERY NUMBER SHOWS WHERE IT CAME FROM mutcd 11th edition · cdot s-630-1

Where did that number come from?

Every taper, buffer and spacing on the plan carries its citation in the audit: the MUTCD section, table or figure, or the CDOT S-630-1 sheet it came from. The person who reviews the plan can check it in one step. Where a value needs a person, the plan says so.

WHAT IT WON'T DO
It won't stamp a plan.

A Certified TCS or PE reviews and signs every one.

It won't promise approval.

That's the city's call.

It won't guess quietly.

A value it inferred is marked as inferred, and anything it couldn't check is listed as not verified.

WHY WE'RE BUILDING IT

The first national manual on traffic control devices came out in 1935. Every edition since has added what the last one learned. Applying it to one road at one speed is careful work every single time.

Two of us come from the field. We built the tool we kept wishing we had: it applies the manual to your road, shows where every number came from, and leaves the judgment calls to the licensed people who make them.
